<Hydrogen generation during serpentinization is strongly controlled by mineral specific surface area.

Highly alkaline fluids significantly enhance serpentinization kinetics and hydrogen yield.

Gold capsule experiments are effective to study governing factors for serpentinization reactions.

Engineered fracture networks and reaction-driven cracking are critical for field-scale stimulation of geologic hydrogen.>
